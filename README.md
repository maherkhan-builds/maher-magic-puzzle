# ✦ Maher Magic Puzzle

**Turn ANY image into an interactive sliding puzzle — right in your browser.**

🎮 **Play it now:** https://maherkhan-builds.github.io/maher-magic-puzzle/

Upload a photo from your phone gallery, your desktop, Pinterest — anything — and it
instantly becomes a sliding puzzle you can play. Made for kids, adults, and brands.

## ✨ Features

- 🖼️ **Your image, your puzzle** — tap *Change image* (or drag & drop a file) and play with any picture. Your image is saved in your browser, so your puzzle is truly *yours*.
- 🔊 **Satisfying slide sounds** — every tile slides with a real "snap" click, generated live with the Web Audio API (no audio files needed).
- 🎉 **Win celebration** — confetti party, victory fanfare and claps when you solve it, plus a best-score tracker per level.
- 🎚️ **Levels** — Beginner 3×3, Advanced 4×4, Expert 5×5. Too hard? Drop back down — it should always feel solvable.
- 👀 **Hold to peek** — press and hold to see the full picture when you're stuck.
- 🔢 **Easy mode** — toggle tile numbers on for young kids.
- 📱 **Works everywhere** — tap on touchscreens, click with a mouse. Fully responsive.
- 🧩 **Always solvable** — puzzles are shuffled with real moves, so every game has a solution.

## 🏷️ For brands & websites

The whole game is **one single `index.html` file** with zero dependencies.
Drop it into any website, swap in a product image, and you have an interactive
brand experience (great for product pages, campaigns, and event microsites).
To embed it in an existing page:

```html
<iframe src="https://maherkhan-builds.github.io/maher-magic-puzzle/"
        width="420" height="900" style="border:0;border-radius:20px"></iframe>
```
